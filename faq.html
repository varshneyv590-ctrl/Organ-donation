Organ Donation FAQs

Can anyone donate organs? +
Most people can become organ donors regardless of age. Medical professionals evaluate the donor's health and suitability at the time of donation.
Is there any cost for organ donation? +
No. Organ donation is free for the donor and their family. All medical expenses related to organ recovery are covered by the transplant organization.
Can family members decide later? +
Yes. Family members may be consulted at the time of donation. However, it is important to discuss your donation wishes with your family in advance.
Which organs can be donated? +
Organs that can be donated include the heart, kidneys, liver, lungs, pancreas, intestines, and tissues such as corneas, skin, bones, and heart valves.
