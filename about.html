About Organ Donation

Giving the Gift of Life

What is Organ Donation?

Organ donation is the process of giving an organ or tissue to someone who needs a transplant. It can save and improve lives by replacing damaged or failing organs with healthy ones.

Types of Organ Donation

1. Living Donation

A living person donates an organ or part of an organ, such as a kidney or a part of the liver, to help another person.


2. Deceased Donation

Organs and tissues are donated after a person's death, helping multiple patients receive life-saving transplants.
