Blood Donor Eligibility Guidelines

Check if you are eligible to save lives today

1. Basic Requirements & Age Limits

🎂 Age Criteria

Standard Range: 18 to 65 years old.

Must be at least 18 years of age to give legal consent for whole blood donation.

⚖️ Weight

Minimum Weight: 45 kg – 50 kg (depending on local regulations).

Ensures donor blood volume can safely handle standard unit extraction.

🩸 Hemoglobin & Vital Signs

  • Hb Level: Minimum 12.5 g/dL
  • Blood Pressure: 100-140/60-90 mmHg
  • Pulse: 50 to 100 bpm

2. Medical & Health Conditions

Eligible / Allowed

Allowed Conditions

  • High BP: Allowed if controlled on daily medication.
  • Diabetes: Allowed if controlled via oral medication or diet (non-insulin dependent).
  • Minor Allergies: Allowed if currently asymptomatic.
Temporary Wait Period

Temporary Deferrals

  • Tattoos / Piercings: Wait 6 to 12 months.
  • Antibiotics / Infections: Wait 14 days after completion.
  • Vaccinations: Wait 14 to 28 days depending on type.
  • Pregnancy / Breastfeeding: Wait 12 months post-delivery.
Permanently Ineligible

Permanent Exclusions

  • Chronic Illnesses: Heart disease, kidney disease, or severe asthma.
  • Bloodborne Infections: Positive for HIV, Hepatitis B, or Hepatitis C.
  • Cancer: History of blood cancers (leukemia/lymphoma).

3. Common Myths & Facts

❌ Myth: Donating blood causes permanent physical weakness or low immunity. +
✔ Fact: Your body replaces the lost liquid volume within 24–48 hours, and red blood cells are fully restored within a few weeks. Donating blood does not weaken your immune system.
❌ Myth: You can contract diseases or infections like HIV while donating blood. +
✔ Fact: All equipment used—including needles and blood bags—is completely sterile, single-use, and discarded immediately after donation. There is zero risk of contracting infections.
❌ Myth: Vegetarians or diabetics cannot donate blood. +
✔ Fact: Vegetarians can donate as long as their hemoglobin level meets the minimum requirement (12.5 g/dL). Diabetics whose blood sugar is controlled with oral medication are also eligible.
❌ Myth: Taking daily prescription medications disqualifies you from donating. +
✔ Fact: Many common medications (such as blood pressure pills, birth control, or cholesterol drugs) do not disqualify you. Only specific drugs like blood thinners or acne medications require a wait period.
